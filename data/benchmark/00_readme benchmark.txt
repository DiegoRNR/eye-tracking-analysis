This folder contains data on word frequency and length in the Multilingual Eye-Movements Corpus (MECO), L2 component. The data is based on release 1.0 version 1.0 of MECO-L2 available at https://osf.io/q9h43/. 

Variables are:
- "trialid": Text number (and the trial number in the experiment).
- "sentnum": Sentence number in the text
- "ianum": Word number in the text.
- "ia": Word as presented to the reader.
- "ia_clean": Word converted to lower-case and stripped of punctuation.
- "freq": Word frequency in the fasttext corpus.               
- "zipf_freq": Zipf value of word frequency in the fasttext corpus (see Van Heuven, W. J., Mandera, P., Keuleers, E., & Brysbaert, M. (2014). SUBTLEX-UK: A new and improved word frequency database for British English. Quarterly journal of experimental psychology, 67(6), 1176-1190. for definiton)          
- "length": word length in letters/characters.
